## ALINA OS: an AI-native operating system

A computer with a resident intelligence. You speak or type, and a fleet of agents works
across the whole machine, with rules about what they remember, a visible record of what
they do, review before anything consequential happens, and an OS you can rebuild from text.

- **It knows.** Audience-partitioned memory that fails closed, curated nightly by a
  council of models from different labs. The curator cannot delete.
- **It acts.** The desktop is an API: every agent gets its own workspace, every window
  it opens is signed, every call is audited, and a locked session means read-only.
- **It's checked.** Cross-model review, a desktop that stages human review, exact-action
  approvals, and receipts.
- **It stands on NixOS.** The whole machine is a diff, with atomic rebuilds and one-step
  rollback.
